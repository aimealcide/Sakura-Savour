Who We Are

A Kigali team that knows logistics from the ground up.

Sakura KGL grew out of years spent actually doing this work: standing in customs queues, chasing warehouse stock counts, sitting across the table from clients trying to make sense of their supply chain. That hands-on background is still how we run things today, across our two divisions: freight logistics and business advisory.

We're based at Rwandex on KN 3 Rd in Kigali, and while most of our clients are here in Rwanda, we regularly connect them to partners and routes well beyond it.
